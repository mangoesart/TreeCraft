    About TreeCraft

    What TreeCraft is, who it is for and how it thinks about trees.

    Read time 1 minuteTreeCraft 0.1.0 Beta

    TreeCraft is a procedural tree modeler for games. Instead of sculpting a tree by hand, you describe how it grows. A trunk rises from the ground, branches sprout along the trunk, and leaves cover the branches. TreeCraft builds the 3D model from those rules.

    How TreeCraft thinks about trees

    Every tree is a chain of generators. Each generator makes one kind of part and places it on its parent:

    Generator What it makes
    Tree The root of every tree. It holds global settings such as the shape radius.
    Trunk One or more trunks that grow up from the ground.
    Branch Branches that grow out of a trunk or out of other branches.
    Leaf Leaf cards placed on branches, or on other leaves.

    Change one number on a generator and every part it made updates at once. To give a tree more branches, raise the branch generator's frequency. To make them droop, add gravity.

    Custom generation, no presets

    TreeCraft starts with an empty scene that holds only the Tree node. You build every tree yourself from generators. There are no ready-made tree presets to start from.

    What you can do with TreeCraft

    • Build trees in a node graph in the Generation tab.
    • Edit each part in the Properties panel on the left.
    • Make bark and leaf materials in the Materials tab.
    • Draw leaf shapes, pivots and anchors in the Meshes tab and the cutout editor.
    • Look at the tree in perspective or orthographic views, with many drawing modes.
    • Add wind, season colours, forces and leaf collision.
    • Save your work and export the tree as an OBJ mesh for your game engine.
