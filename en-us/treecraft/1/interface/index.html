    The TreeCraft window

    A tour of the menus, panels, toolbar and tabs.

    Read time 2 minutesTreeCraft 0.1.0 Beta

    The TreeCraft window has four main areas: the menu bar along the top, the Properties panel on the left, the viewport in the middle and the dock on the right.

    Menu bar

    Menu What's in it
    File New, Open, Recent files, Close, Save, Save incremental, Save as, export commands and Exit.
    Edit Undo and Redo (each names the step it will undo or redo), Preferences and window properties.
    Tools Ambient occlusion, collision primitives, scene scale and unit conversion, and asset clean-up.
    Window Show or hide panels, and reset the layout.
    Assets Materials and meshes used by the tree.
    Help Tips and information about TreeCraft.

    Every tree you open gets a tab above the viewport. Use + next to the tabs to start another tree.

    Properties panel

    The Properties panel shows the settings of whatever is selected: a generator, a material or a mesh. When nothing is selected it shows shortcuts:

    • Wind, Lighting, Global properties and Window properties.
    • Asset Browser: your saved assets. Drag one into the Generation tab to add it to the tree.
    • PivotPainter2: settings for exporting pivot data for wind animation in game engines.
    • New tree, Open..., Save and Save As....

    The four buttons at the top of the panel switch what you edit:

    • Generator: the rules of the selected generator (available in this version).
    • Node, Freehand and Export: planned editing modes (see the Release notes).

    Properties are grouped in collapsible sections. To learn how to change them, see Working with properties.

    Viewport and toolbar

    The viewport shows your tree. The toolbar above it has four groups:

    Group Buttons
    View Render (how the tree is drawn), Show (which parts are visible), Zoom (framing the camera).
    Scene Forces, Scene objects, Collision Objects, Wind, Light.
    Edit Generators / Nodes, Season, Add, Visibility, Gizmo.
    Post AO (ambient occlusion), Collision (leaf collision), Export.

    The < button at the end of the toolbar folds it away to save space. The bar under the toolbar switches between the perspective view and the orthographic views. The bottom of the viewport counts triangles, vertices, branches and leaves.

    Read more in The viewport and Wind, season, forces and collision.

    Dock

    The dock on the right has three tabs (switch between them with the tabs at the bottom of the dock):

    • Generation: the node graph where you build the tree. See Building a tree with generators.
    • Materials: bark and leaf materials. See Materials.
    • Meshes: leaf shapes and their levels of detail. See Leaf meshes and the cutout editor.

    To resize the panels, drag the gap between a panel and the viewport.
