    Preferences

    Change colours, viewport options, lighting and wind defaults.

    Read time 1 minuteTreeCraft 0.1.0 Beta

    Open Edit > Preferences to change how TreeCraft looks and behaves. Preferences are saved automatically in %APPDATA%/TreeCraft/prefs.txt.

    Themes

    • 3D Viewport colours: the background (top and bottom), the grid and every fifth grid line, the X and Z axes, labels, bones, normals, scribed edges, forces, collision objects, the picked object and the corner axes.
    • Background brightness: from 0 to 3.
    • A colour picker with a hue bar, a saturation/brightness square, and R, G, B and A values.
    • Presets: save your colours under a name, then load or delete them later.

    Viewport

    Lighting, wireframe, texture mapping, hiding back faces, alpha testing, the grid and view labels. Also:

    • Zoom distance scale: how far the camera stands back when it frames the tree.
    • Manipulator size: how close a click must be to pick a scene object.
    • Snapping: translation, rotation and scale snapping, each with its own step.
    • Field of view.

    Lighting

    The sun's direction and height, ambient occlusion and whether lighting is on.

    Wind

    Whether wind is on, its strength and its direction. The tree sways in that direction.

    Global

    The Tree generator's shape radius, the preview level of detail and tree statistics.
