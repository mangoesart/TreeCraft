    Working with properties

    Property tabs, number fields, variance and curves.

    Read time 3 minutesTreeCraft 0.1.0 Beta

    When you click a generator in the Generation tab, its settings appear in the Properties panel on the left. This page explains how the panel is organised and how to change values.

    Property tabs

    Generators have many settings, so they are sorted into tabs.

    Branch and trunk generators

    Tab What it controls
    Gen Generation: mode, frequency, where children appear.
    Spine The centre line: length, start angle, gravity, noise, kinks and forces.
    Shape Shape control.
    Skin Radius, taper, flare and the type of surface.
    UV How the bark texture wraps around the branch.
    Displacement Surface displacement settings.
    Material Material, season and vertex colour settings.
    Vertex Color Colours stored on the mesh's vertices for use in game shaders.
    Animation Wind and growth.
    Segments How many sides and steps the mesh has.
    LOD Level-of-detail settings.
    Hand Drawn Settings for branches drawn by hand.

    Leaf generators

    Tab What it controls
    Gen Generation and anchors.
    Skin Leaf size, lighting and deformation.
    Orientation Facing the sky, droop and final adjustments.
    Lighting Lighting and material settings.
    Material Season, leaf type and vertex colour.
    LOD Level-of-detail settings.
    Animation Wind and growth.
    Collision Leaf collision.

    The All tab lists everything, including random seeds. Names shown faintly are kept with the tree and saved in your file, but don't change the model in this version yet.

    Changing a value

    • Drag across a number field to change it smoothly, like turning a dial.
    • Click a number to select it, then type a new value. Press Ctrl+A or double-click to select it again.
    • Use the arrows to step the value up or down. Whole numbers step by 1. Other values step by a round amount (such as 0.01 or 0.5) that suits the setting.
    • Choices (such as Mode or Style) open a list.

    Every finished change can be undone with Ctrl+Z and redone with Ctrl+Y. Undo has no limit.

    Variance

    Rows with a +/- button can vary from part to part, so no two branches look exactly the same. Open Variance Settings with that button:

    Setting Meaning
    Variance How far each part may stray from the value.
    Distribution Uniform (any amount is equally likely) or Gaussian (most parts stay close to the value).
    Cohesion From Cohesive (neighbouring parts vary together) to Random (each part varies on its own).
    Scale, Offset The size and starting point of the cohesive pattern.

    Curves

    Some properties can change along the tree with a curve. Open the curve editor from the property's row.

    • A Parent curve changes the value depending on where the part sits on its parent.
    • A Distribution (blue) or Profile (yellow) curve changes the value along the part's own length.

    In the curve editor you can pick a preset shape (Max, Min, Linear growth, Exp decay, Bell, S-Curve, Droop and more), draw freely, or move points by hand. Right-click for more commands: insert a smooth, step or corner point, delete points, flip or scale the curve, and copy or paste curves. A curve always keeps at least two points.

    UV mapping

    The UV tab controls how bark wraps the branch:

    • Style: Unrestricted, U locked to 0–1, or U and V locked to 0–1.
    • U Absolute and Relative set how many times the texture repeats around the branch. Offset and Twist turn it.
    • V Style sets how the texture runs along the branch: Absolute, Relative keeping the aspect ratio, or Relative to the branch's length.
    • Random flip flips the twist on about half of the branches.
