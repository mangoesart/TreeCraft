    Generation modes

    How Classic, Absolute, Interval and Phyllotaxy decide where children grow.

    Read time 2 minutesTreeCraft 0.1.0 Beta

    A generator's Mode (in the Gen tab of its properties) decides how many children it makes and where they sit on the parent. This page covers the four main modes.

    Classic

    Makes a number of children on each parent and scatters them between First and Last.

    • Style: Absolute makes the whole-number part of Frequency on each parent.
    • Style: Relative makes more children on longer parents (parent length × Frequency × 0.025).
    • Fractions are dropped, not rounded.
    • Balanced spaces the children evenly around the parent. Without it they point in random directions.
    • Offset adds randomness to the spacing. Sweep limits how far around the parent they spread (1 = all the way round). Roll turns them all together.

    Absolute

    Makes exactly Number children on each parent, spaced evenly around it. Jumble adds randomness. Sweep and Roll work as in Classic. A trunk usually uses Absolute with Number 1.

    Interval

    Walks down the parent from Last to First in regular steps and puts a cluster of children at each step:

    • Frequency sets how often a step happens (a higher value means shorter steps).
    • Count is the number of children at each step, spread evenly around the parent.
    • Spiral turns each step a little further round than the last.
    • Spread jitters the children along the parent.
    • Limit caps the number of steps.

    When you switch a generator to Interval, TreeCraft converts its frequency so the tree keeps a similar density.

    Phyllotaxy

    Places children at regular nodes along the parent, the way real plants arrange leaves. Settings: internode length (distance between nodes), children per node, spread, spiral and limit.

    Choosing a mode

    You want Use
    A natural scatter of branches Classic
    One trunk, or an exact count Absolute
    Whorls of branches at regular heights, like a conifer Interval
    Leaves in pairs or spirals along a twig Phyllotaxy

    Note: The Generation seed changes the random choices without changing the rules. Use Randomize to try a new seed.
