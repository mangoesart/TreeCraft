    The viewport

    Look at your tree in perspective or orthographic views and change how it is drawn.

    Read time 2 minutesTreeCraft 0.1.0 Beta

    The viewport in the middle of the window shows your tree as you build it. It opens in a Perspective view. You can switch to flat orthographic views when you need exact side or top views.

    Moving the camera

    Action Mouse or key
    Orbit around the tree Left-drag
    Pan Middle-drag or Shift+drag
    Zoom Right-drag or mouse wheel
    Frame the whole tree F, or Zoom > Selected

    Views

    Use the menu under the toolbar, or the number pad:

    View Number pad
    Perspective 0
    Front 1
    Right 3
    Top 7
    Switch perspective/orthographic 5

    Front, Back, Left, Right, Top and Bottom are orthographic: there is no perspective, so sizes and angles can be measured by eye. The axis widget in the corner shows which way X, Y and Z point.

    Render modes

    Render changes how the tree is drawn. Standard shows the finished look. Other modes help you check your work:

    • Scribed: draws the triangle edges over a plain surface.
    • AO: shows only the ambient occlusion.
    • Albedo Check and Subsurface Check: colour values that are too dark show in blue, and values that are too bright show in red.
    • Lighting: shows one part of the lighting (untextured, diffuse, specular, ambient, subsurface or shadow).
    • Material: shows one material slot (colour, opacity, gloss, normal and so on).
    • Vertex Colors: shows the colour, or the red, green or blue channel alone.
    • Other: classic shading models (Phong, Blinn-Phong, Oren-Nayar, Lambert, Flat) and diagnostic views (depth, overdraw, texture coordinates and seams).

    Show

    Show turns parts on and off: leaves (key 1), branches (3), the grid, bones (the centre line of every branch), normals, and two-sided rendering.

    Zoom and visibility

    • Zoom: frame everything or only the selection, and copy, paste or reset the camera.
    • Visibility: hide the selected parts (H), isolate them (I), or show everything again.

    Light

    Light turns lighting on or off and opens the light's properties, such as the sun's direction and height.

    Statistics

    The bottom of the viewport counts Triangles, Vertices, Branches and Leaves. Keep an eye on them to stay within your game's budget.

    Tip: The ? button at the bottom left of the viewport shows quick tips.
