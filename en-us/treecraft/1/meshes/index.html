    Leaf meshes and the cutout editor

    Shape leaf cards, set their pivot and anchors, and manage levels of detail.

    Read time 4 minutesTreeCraft 0.1.0 Beta

    Leaves in TreeCraft are flat cards or small meshes. In the Meshes tab you choose each leaf's shape, how many points it has at each level of detail, and where it attaches to the branch.

    The top row

    • Drag handle: drop on the viewport to give the selected Leaf generator (or the first one) this mesh.
    • Mesh picker: the mesh you're editing.
    • +/-: opens the Meshes window to add, duplicate, rename, reorder or remove meshes. Changes apply when you press OK.
    • +: loads an OBJ file as a new mesh.
    • Copy, Paste and Duplicate.

    New meshes get unique names automatically: "Mesh", "Mesh 2", "Mesh 3" and so on.

    Levels of detail

    Each mesh has three levels, High, Med and Low, so distant trees can use simpler leaves. Each level is either a cutout drawn in TreeCraft or an OBJ file:

    • ... (Browse) loads a file into the level. When you load a High file, TreeCraft can look for matching "lod1", "lod2" files next to it.
    • Reload reads the file again.
    • Export saves the level as an OBJ file.
    • An empty level uses the level above it. More lets another mesh supply the levels below Low.

    The V: and F: numbers count the vertices and faces. A: counts the anchors.

    Transform and other settings

    Setting Meaning
    Orient Which way is up in the file: Z-Up or Y-Up, right- or left-handed, or any axis order.
    Flip Mirror the mesh on X, Y, Z or a combination.
    Flip normals / Fix winding Repair meshes that look inside-out.
    Wind direction Growth: the axis that points along the leaf (+Y by default). Ripple: the direction used for wind ripples.
    Proxy collision Scale and weight kept for game use.
    Collection Builds the mesh from the branches marked "Contribute to collections".

    The cutout editor

    Choose Edit Mesh... to open Edit Cutout, where you draw the leaf's outline over its texture.

    Meshes list

    The list on the left shows every mesh with its point count. Click one to edit it, or choose New mesh to start another.

    Auto cutout

    Auto cutout traces the outline of the opaque parts of the texture for you. Re-generate traces it again after you change the settings:

    Setting Meaning
    Alpha How opaque a pixel must be to count as part of the leaf.
    Join gap Joins separate parts that are closer than this.
    Min size Ignores specks smaller than this.
    Points How many outline points to use.
    Margin Extra space kept around the leaf.
    Concavity How closely the outline follows inward curves.
    Skip straight parts Leaves out points on straight stretches.
    Variant Tries a different version of the outline.

    Export

    Saves the mesh as FBX, OBJ or XML, with its origin at the pivot and growing along +Y. Choose Export mesh... to pick where to save it.

    Points

    • Click outside the shape to add an outline point. It joins the nearest point without crossing the outline.
    • Click inside the shape to add an inner point that gives the leaf more detail.
    • Drag points to move them. Use x to remove points and R to reset them (TreeCraft asks before resetting).

    Pivot

    The Pivot (white, with an orange angle line) is where the leaf attaches to the branch and the direction it grows. Drag it, or set its X, Y and angle in the Pivot group.

    Anchors

    Green anchors mark spots where more leaves can grow. A Leaf generator placed under another Leaf generator grows one leaf on each green anchor, pointing along the anchor's line. Right-click an anchor to give it a number. The child generator's Anchors setting can then use all anchors or only the ones with a given number.

    If no anchor matches, the status bar shows: "Unable to find anchor for node". Check the anchors (on every level), First/Last and the anchor number.

    Transform and Color

    • M, R and S move, rotate and scale the whole card, including its pivot and anchors, with an on-screen gizmo.
    • P paints vertex colours with a brush. The colours tint the leaf on the tree. The swatch picks the colour, and R resets the colours after asking.

    Options

    • Tessellation adds evenly spread inner points for smoother bending.
    • Angle turns the leaf's growth line.
    • Double-sided adds back faces.
    • Improve folding rearranges triangles so the leaf folds cleanly along its midrib.

    Levels

    On the right, the arrow buttons copy the canvas into a level (->) or load a level onto the canvas (<-). Each level keeps its own outline, colours and anchors.

    Middle-drag to pan the canvas, and middle+left drag to zoom. Press Confirm to keep your changes or Cancel to discard them.
